Calendar rules

Year 1 began on 24 September 1998. Each new year starts on 24 September in your device's local calendar. The 12 named months have 30 days each. The remaining 5 or 6 complementary days fall after Stellus and before the next Skyrus. These are provisionally named Intercalary Day 1–6; you can rename them later.

Each month has three 10-day décades, with days named Indominus, Indus, Scorpius, Venus, Rapidashus, Hous, Fernus, Epus, Tuskus and Amyctus. The clock hands move continuously: the hour hand makes one revolution per day, the minute hand one per decimal hour, and the second hand one per decimal minute. The decimal clock starts at local midnight and uses 10 hours/day, 100 minutes/hour and 100 seconds/minute. One decimal second = 0.864 ordinary seconds.

This is a custom anniversary-based calendar, not the historical equinox-based French Republican calendar. The clock follows your iPhone's local time zone and daylight-saving setting.
